# Modules

## Available Now

### Words Practice

Speaking practice for Azerbaijani prompts. Records learning events, wrong answers, skipped items, XP, and review queue entries.

### Sentence Practice

Includes Repeat English Sentences and Translate & Speak. Records learning events, wrong answers, skipped items, XP, and review queue entries.

### Grammar Practice

Typing-only grammar practice. Records learning events, wrong answers, skipped items, XP, and review queue entries.

### Writing Practice

Typing-only writing practice with:

- Azerbaijani -> English
- Fix the English
- Listen & Write

Records learning events, wrong answers, skipped items, XP, and review queue entries.

### Mistake Review

Central review queue for due mistakes across available modules.
